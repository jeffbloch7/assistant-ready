Safe by design

  • You stay in control. You share your screen and do the typing. I never take control of your computer.
  • I never ask for passwords. Not your passwords, security codes or payment details. Ever.
  • Only what you need. We connect only the tools you'll actually use, with careful permission settings.
  • Employee data stays protected. HR clients use a company-approved plan and a clear list of what never goes in.
  • Scam awareness. Anthropic will never phone you or ask for access to your computer. Anyone who does is a scammer.

Questions

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. If you can use email and join a video call, you're ready. We go at your pace, in plain English.

What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. You can talk to it like a very capable helper: it writes, researches, summarizes, organizes and can work with your files and apps. Claude Code is a more advanced version that can build things for you, like simple tools and websites.

What do I need to buy?

A Claude plan, paid directly to Anthropic. Claude Pro at $20 a month is right for most people. If you're using it for work, check first whether your company already has a Claude plan. I'll help you choose before we meet.

Mac or Windows?

Either works. You'll need a laptop or desktop computer (not a tablet) for the session. The Claude phone app is a nice extra.

What if it doesn't work for me?

If you're not up and running by the end of our session, you get a full refund.

Are you affiliated with Anthropic?

No. Assistant Ready is an independent business. I'm a daily Claude user who helps others get set up.
